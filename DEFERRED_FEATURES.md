# JANUS deferred features

This file records intentionally deferred work so later sessions do not accidentally treat it as missing or silently enable it.

## Ambient physical-world sensing

Continuous/ambient microphone and camera capture remain **deferred**. PR #52's continuing-input design is a digital-world observation system: governed web/YouTube discovery plus the existing explicit user-selected file/image/audio/action senses. It does not authorize covert or continuous physical-world recording.

A future ambient-sensing proposal would require a separate owner decision covering privacy, permission UX, battery/data use, OS background limits, visible capture indicators, retention rules, and per-user opt-in/opt-out behavior.

## Rich background visual deliberation

Background cores generating images for one another, candidate-render/review loops, automatic visual revisions, unrestricted spontaneous rendering and other multi-core image-conversation features remain deferred until income comfortably exceeds their cost and the owner explicitly enables them.

Current image generation remains bounded to user-requested and rare explanatory visuals under the existing image-generation policy.

## Cross-platform release expansion

Windows and iOS/macOS release work remains deferred until the Android release process, signing, production deployment and real-device soak behavior are understood and stable.

## Premium/cloud voice

Higher-cost cloud voice quality and cross-platform spoken-reply parity remain later work. Android explicit push-to-talk/device-native speech behavior is the current baseline.

## Autonomous code modification

JANUS may diagnose, propose and persist maintenance requests, but autonomous source editing, package installation, model/API changes, merging and deployment remain out of scope. Owner/ChatGPT Supervisor approval remains mandatory.

## Provider/model upgrades

Model/provider changes should be proposed through the maintenance system and evaluated for quality, compatibility and cost. They must not silently raise the owner-set research budget or other cost ceilings.

## Research budget expansion

The default web-research policy remains US$20/month total with US$10/month autonomous/background. Raising either amount is deferred until the owner explicitly asks for a change.
